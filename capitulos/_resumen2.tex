\section*{Abstract.}

This project aims to develop an information system for the management and control of processes in a vehicle verification center located in Mexico City. The system is designed to optimize activities related to vehicle inspection, such as user registration, appointment scheduling, verification control, and results storage.
The proposal arises from the need to improve the operational efficiency of verification centers, reduce errors in information handling, and ensure proper control of vehicle emissions. To achieve this, a solution based on web development technologies and database systems is proposed, enabling process automation and fast, secure access to information.
The system considers the interaction of different types of users, including administrators, technicians, and clients, ensuring data integrity and availability through a structured model. Additionally, it aims to support decision-making by generating reports and tracking completed verifications.
This development seeks to contribute to strengthening vehicle control processes and compliance with current environmental regulations, providing a technological tool that enhances service quality in verification centers and supports the reduction of environmental impact.
