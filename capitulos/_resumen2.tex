\section*{Abstract}

Strategic decision-making in higher education institutions depends on the timely, centralized, and reliable availability of their key metrics. At the Universidad Politécnica de Texcoco, the use of independent spreadsheets is insufficient given the growth in enrollment. Data dispersion causes constant errors, hinders historical analysis, and prevents the early detection of urgent problems such as school dropout or low academic performance. Given the lack of a specialized computing tool to mitigate these limitations, this work presents the development of a comprehensive web system for the semi-automated entry, statistical processing, and dynamic visualization of priority indicators, including terminal efficiency, failure rates, and teacher evaluation, implemented with technologies such as FastAPI in the backend, Pandas for data analysis, and tools like Celery and Redis for asynchronous processing. By structuring historical data in a relational model on PostgreSQL and implementing interactive dashboards under secure access schemes, the solution eradicates the dependence on manual navigation between files, ensuring information integrity and providing the Academic Direction with an efficient analytical infrastructure for evidence-based decision-making.

\vspace{5cm}\textbf{Keywords:} (FastAPI, Pandas, Celery, Redis, PostgreSQL, Indicators.)
