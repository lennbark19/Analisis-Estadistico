\section*{Abstract}

The management of large amounts of information in the Academic Directorate of the Universidad Politécnica de Texcoco has complicated analysis and decision-making over time. The manual handling of data in spreadsheets caused delays, recording errors, and made it difficult to monitor key indicators such as enrollment figures, student performance, dropout rates, completion rates, and degree attainment per generation. To address this issue, this work describes the creation of an Academic Statistical Analysis Platform. This is a digital tool where all information is centralized, facilitating the ingestion of historical data to analyze and present it clearly and graphically.

The project was organized systematically to progress step by step. The internal architecture of the system is responsible for receiving, structuring, and storing information securely, processing large volumes of data without delays. On the other hand, the user interface was designed to be interactive, providing visual dashboards that are fast and intuitive to understand, even when analyzing multiple years of data. The final outcome eliminates administrative bottlenecks and provides university authorities with a reliable tool to assess institutional status in real time, supporting data-driven decision-making to enhance educational quality.
